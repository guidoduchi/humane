# Gungnir

Gungnir is Odrin's future spear, forged from the core and wood of the Tree of Infinity after a Singularity fragment merges with a tree.

## Canon notes

- The tree is not divine in origin; it is a living organism altered by a neutral fragment.
- Odrin mistakes the phenomenon for infinite knowledge.
- The exact function of Gungnir remains pending final definition.
