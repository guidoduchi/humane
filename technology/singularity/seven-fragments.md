# Seven Fragments

The Singularity fragments intentionally into seven total pieces. Six are physical fragments scattered across Earth. The seventh is a field rather than a physical object.

## Engineering logic

The seven-part structure is tied to the Singularity's layered icosahedral architecture and Fibonacci-weighted engineering. The system has twenty faces and one core, giving it twenty-one logic regions. The seven-fragment division is the smallest stable unfolding that can preserve the whole system while creating the gap needed for host compatibility.

## Narrative role

The fragments are neutral. Each one has the computational capability to alter physical, chemical, or biological phenomena. These are not separate categories of magic; they are different scales of the same underlying physics.

## Open design note

The exact function of each fragment is still pending final definition. The current known structure is:

1. Sharrah Pool fragment / core command interface.
2. A fragment capable of merging with a tree, later misread by Odrin as the Tree of Infinity.
3. Additional physical fragments to be assigned.
4. The nonphysical field fragment, likely tied to relationship, identity, or coherence between the pieces.
