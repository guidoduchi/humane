# Star Piece

The Star Piece is the fragment that falls into ancient Sharrah Pool at the climax of _Awakening_. To the children, it appears as a falling star. In truth, it is one piece of the Singularity's intentionally fragmented system.

## Fragmentation

The Star Piece does not break because Earth's atmosphere damages it. Its descent is intentional and tied to the Host Consciousness Arrest Protocol. The Singularity divides into seven fragments as part of a Fibonacci-weighted, layered icosahedral system.

## Sharrah Pool fragment

The fragment in Sharrah Pool contains enough command structure to initiate a host sync. When Erlen touches it underwater, the protocol begins. Muyu's interruption and Ollie's identity-anchor prevent Erlen from being fully arrested and overwritten.

## Symbolic role

The Star Piece is not magic entering the world. It is technology so far beyond Bronze Age understanding that it becomes myth by lack of language.
