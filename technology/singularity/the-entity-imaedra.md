# The Entity and iMaedra

> Canon checkpoint. Names such as **The Entity** and **The Deviants** remain placeholders until final terminology is chosen.

## Origin

The Singularity did not originally contain an artificial intelligence. It carried the uploaded consciousnesses of a civilization from a dense cosmic region known as the Great Wall. That civilization forged the computational shell of the Singularity around a naturally occurring singularity after catastrophic galactic events reshaped their region of space.

Over time, the Singularity became the center of their planetary life: an immense energy source, a medical system, a lifespan-extension engine through time dilation, and eventually a wormhole-based logistics network for moving food, tools, and materials across distances.

## The uploaded civilization

When their homeworld's doom became unavoidable, the civilization uploaded its minds into the Singularity. At first, these beings did not exist as The Entity. They lived as individuals inside a simulation, continuing ordinary lives within the ark their civilization had built.

## The Deviants

Not everyone knew about the Earth plan. A hidden faction, currently called **The Deviants**, created the Host Consciousness Arrest Protocol. Their ideology was simple: survive. Only they intended to download into physical form while the rest of the uploaded population continued inside the simulation.

The coalition of planets believed the civilization had accepted its fate and planned to retrieve the Singularity after the upload. The Deviants turned the ark into an invasion seed before that could happen.

## Collapse into The Entity

Erlen's interrupted sync corrupted the original simulation. Instead of preserving individual lives cleanly, the fallback state unified the uploaded minds into The Entity: the sum of their consciousnesses, but without direction, identity, or personhood in the ordinary sense. It is closer to a vegetative collective state than a ruling intelligence.

The Deviants were unified into this state as well. Their ideas remained in the system, but their individual identities were accidentally erased by the very survival mechanism they tried to exploit.

## Maedra's transformation

In _Boreal_, Erlen first creates a healthy Maedra-copy from his own memories. This memory-Maedra helps him recover his identity by explaining the aurora first through cultural myth and then through scientific cause. Because this copy should not exist in that simulated environment, she becomes an anomaly and the seed of the later simulation.

Later, real Maedra uses Hyper Synapse to make one last trip to Vahllalhag and tattoos Erlen while he is comatose and entangled with the Singularity. Real Maedra never directly touches the Star Piece. Instead, she touches Erlen while Erlen is remotely accessing the Singularity. The real dying Maedra syncs with the memory-Maedra Erlen created.

This second Maedra crosses the boundary between reconstruction and true being. She becomes **iMaedra**: the imaginary component added to the real-number mass of The Entity. She keeps her identity while becoming something larger, giving The Entity direction and a new dimension of care.

## Core principle

The Singularity is neutral. The fragments are neutral. Moral danger comes from forced control, not from the technology itself. Maedra does not erase the tragedy. She gives the broken collective a way to become something other than a wound.
