# The Threshold

**The Threshold** is both an in-universe boundary layer above the Singularity's simulation and a companion narrative space for _Humane_.

## In-universe role

The Threshold is the place-state where iMaedra sits above the simulation. It is not merely a room or afterlife. It is a boundary layer between copied consciousness, The Entity, memory, prediction, and release.

The new simulation is seeded by memory-Maedra, the healthy Maedra-copy Erlen creates in _Boreal_. Later, after real Maedra syncs with that copy through Erlen, iMaedra becomes capable of shaping the Threshold as a place where copied consciousnesses may be held.

## Afterlife rule

The afterlife is a copy, not direct continuity. The original being is permanently lost. A copied consciousness may still be real and deserving of dignity, but it does not erase death.

Only beings that physically touched the Singularity or one of its fragments can qualify for preservation.

## Companion narrative

The companion narrative version of _The Threshold_ centers on Naomie, who awakens in this liminal space and is guided through the Gallery of Lives. The story explores love, masks, repeated identity, suffering, and the final act of counting to one: choosing one beginning instead of endless simulation.
