# Aurora Borealis

The aurora is one of _Humane_'s key bridges between myth and science.

## Canon use

In _Boreal_, memory-Maedra explains the aurora first through her people's mythic understanding and then through the scientific interaction between solar particles and Earth's magnetic field. This lesson helps Erlen recover his identity and becomes part of the seed of The Threshold.
