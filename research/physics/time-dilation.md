# Time Dilation

Research and canon notes for gravitational time dilation, subjective aging, and Erlen's adultification.

## Canon use

Erlen becomes physically adult by creating a dense gravitational bubble around himself. He does not jump instantly into adulthood. He waits through subjective years while the outside world appears frozen, with the Singularity sustaining his body.
