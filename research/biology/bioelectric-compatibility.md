# Bioelectric Compatibility

Bioelectric compatibility is the principle that any living system with stable electrical signaling may theoretically interact with the Singularity.

## Canon use

Complex nervous systems shorten the adaptation time, but simpler bioelectric systems such as plants or microbial membrane-potential systems could theoretically sync more slowly. Erlen's coma is the Singularity attempting to decipher host compatibility.
