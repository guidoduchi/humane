# Ollie

Ollie is Erlen's female dog and one of the quiet emotional anchors of _Humane_.

## Canon notes

- Ollie helps Erlen remember who he is during the corrupted sync.
- Her memory of Erlen matters because it is simple, embodied, non-linguistic, and rooted in affection.
- She represents one of the hidden victories of love against the Host Consciousness Arrest Protocol.
