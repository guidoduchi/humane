# Pigloo

Pigloo is Katlas's dog and a loyal presence in the human-animal texture of _Humane_.

## Canon notes

- Pigloo belongs to the world of working ancient hunting dogs rather than polished modern breeds.
- His presence helps keep danger, humor, and animal loyalty close to the ground.
