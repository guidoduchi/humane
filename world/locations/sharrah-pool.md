# Sharrah Pool

Sharrah Pool is the ancient river pool below Mel Tor where the Star Piece falls at the climax of _Awakening_.

## Canon notes

- It is part of a river environment, not a fantasy lake.
- The setting should feel ancient, cold, forested, rocky, and physically real.
- Erlen's underwater contact with the Star Piece initiates the corrupted sync.
