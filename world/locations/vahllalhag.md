# Vahllalhag

Vahllalhag is the compact Pritani teaching settlement at the heart of early _Humane_. It is not larger than Grimspound in footprint; the reinterpretation happens vertically and culturally, through roofing, human activity, teaching use, smoke, ritual, and daily movement.

## Canon notes

- Teaching happens mostly outdoors.
- Interior structures support sleeping, storage, and busy seasonal gatherings.
- The settlement should remain physically compact and grounded in Bronze Age practicality.
