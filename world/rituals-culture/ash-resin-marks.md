# Ash and Resin Marks

Ash and resin marks are provisional identity and protection markings used for children and ritual preparation in _Humane_.

## Canon notes

- Ash is culturally powerful because fire is universal, practical, and sacred-adjacent.
- Resin helps the marks cling to skin and gives the ritual a physical, scented presence.
- These marks contrast with permanent adult tattoos.
