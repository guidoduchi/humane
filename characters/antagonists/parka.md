# Parka

Parka begins as a grieving bear and becomes one of the central opposing forces of Book 1 through Singularity-mediated transformation.

## Canon notes

- Her transformation is not evil magic; it is biological adaptation through neutral Singularity capability.
- Her grief and survival drive become part of the instruction the Singularity interprets.
