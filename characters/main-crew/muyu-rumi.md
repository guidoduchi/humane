# Muyu Rumi

Muyu Rumi is a young girl from the Machalilla culture whose instinctive rescue of Erlen interrupts the Singularity's host sync.

## Canon notes

- Connected to Hyper Instinct.
- Her choice to help a drowning stranger becomes one of the hidden victories that lets humanity survive.
- Her interruption corrupts the sync but saves Erlen from complete consciousness arrest.
