# Alessia Odrinlyr

Alessia Odrinlyr is Odrin's daughter, a strategic young character tied to Hyper Fabric, recovery, persuasion, and the dangerous elegance of fast problem-solving.

## Canon notes

- Formerly tracked under the name Ayka in older notes.
- Her mind excels at breaking problems into usable parts.
- Her strength can look like manipulation when viewed without context.
