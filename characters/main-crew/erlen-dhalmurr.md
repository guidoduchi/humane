# Erlen Dhalmurr

Erlen Dhalmurr is a young Pritani boy, son of Senara, tied to Hyper Focus and the first corrupted sync with the Singularity.

## Canon notes

- Central point-of-view figure for the opening movement of _Humane_.
- Does not become important because the world revolves around him; he becomes important because he survives an impossible accident of history, love, and cosmic machinery.
- After Book 1, he becomes physically adult through subjective time inside a gravitational bubble.
