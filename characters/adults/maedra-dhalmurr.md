# Maedra Dhalmurr

Maedra Dhalmurr is Erlen's grandmother, former matriarch, bearer of Hyper Synapse, and the human origin point of iMaedra.

## Canon notes

- In _Boreal_, Erlen creates a healthy copy of Maedra from memory.
- Real Maedra later tattoos Erlen while he is comatose and entangled with the Singularity.
- Real Maedra syncs with memory-Maedra before dying, becoming a true being born from memory, contact, and identity.
- As iMaedra, she sits above the simulation in The Threshold.
