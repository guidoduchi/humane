# Katlas Vhormirr

Katlas Vhormirr is a rugged protector whose dry humor, resilience, and sacrifice become one of the secret hinges of humanity's survival.

## Canon notes

- Later connected to Hyper Regeneration.
- Removes the Host Consciousness Arrest Protocol by instructing the Singularity through its interface layer.
- Because his consciousness remains scattered when the protocol is removed, his memories remain broken among the fragments.
- Erlen's first fragment-retrieval motive is to revive Katlas, before learning to let him move peacefully into the afterlife.
